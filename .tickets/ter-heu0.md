# Find and reduce grain on rough surfaces under grazing light

Investigate rough surfaces lit at a shallow angle by a direct source or by indirect illumination. Use several second-cut shots, starting with the cabin floor during boarding and the berth floor during work and deck. Compare steep and grazing incidence on the same finish. Record the viewing angle separately.

## Acceptance Criteria

Confirm affected patches in multiple shots. Hold camera, material and exposure fixed while varying incidence. Isolate direct diffuse, direct specular, indirect diffuse and reflected light. Compare consecutive uncompressed frames and settled crops before and after the correction. Preserve mean brightness, material roughness and normal-map detail.
