# A render pass that draws nothing costs about 0.075 ms of the M1 Max's frame, more than the full back-face pass in the cabin

**2026-11-25 · Retired.** This optimization was scoped to an earlier GPU workload. Reopen against a named film segment if its pass dominates a repeatable GPU timing there.

Found by the crowd cost experiment (docs/research/back-face-depth-layer/stage-2/crowd-cost.md, part 1). The probe's side 'empty' begins and ends the back-face pass's three rendering instances, with their clears and barriers, and draws nothing. On the M1 Max it costs +0.074 ms of the frame in the crowd and +0.076 ms in the cabin. The full pass costs +0.041 ms in the cabin, so the pass with its draws costs less than the pass without them. On the RTX 2000 Ada the same side costs 0.007 ms. The cause is unknown. One candidate is the cost of an empty Metal render pass under MoltenVK. It matters for the real back-face pass (t1-j03y): three depth ranges give three rendering instances per frame. Check whether a range with no draw can skip its rendering instance.
