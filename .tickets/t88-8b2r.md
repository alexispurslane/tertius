# The shuttle's main plume leaves one nozzle on the hull's center line, but the Lunar Shuttle model has two side nozzles

**2026-11-25 · Folded into ter-2erh.** Keep the known single-emitter/twin-nozzle mismatch as an ignition control. The source of the discs in the approach remains open.

Measured on ter-9wwx (2026-11-25): the Lunar Shuttle model, at Shuttle.ArtScale 1.6 and Shuttle.ArtPlace, has its two engine nozzles at (±4.56, 0, 13.70) in the shuttle's axes. The main engine (ShuttleJets.MainEnginePlace) and the drawn nozzle (Shuttle.NozzlePose) lie on the line of thrust through the center of mass, (0, -0.27, 13.77).

Visible effect: in the launch and the transfer burn, the plume leaves the middle of the model's tail, between its two nozzles, 4.56 m from each, and the model's nozzles stay dark.

Wanted: two plumes, one from each model nozzle, each with half the thrust, so that their sum stays on the line of thrust through the center of mass. Then no drawn gray-box nozzle with the art.
