# Nothing guards the register count of the key and fill direct shader on Apple's GPU: a merge of main raised it from 115 to 116 and removed the whole gain

Found in t2-vsqh on 2026-11-14 (ARCHITECTURE.md, 'Key and fill'). On Apple's GPU, direct_key_fill.frag keeps 448 resident threads while its highest register is 115 or lower. At register 116 the GPU keeps 384 threads, and the direct pass under key and fill costs about 0.36 ms more on the deck, which is the whole gain of key and fill there.

The merge of main on 2026-11-14 brought the specular target as 8-bit counts (specularAt in gbuffer_inputs.glsl). That change alone raised the shader from register 115 to 116, and no test and no tool reported it. A timed take on the Mac found it: the corner and tile passes plus the direct pass took 3.85 ms against 3.83 ms with every light pair, where the same take before the merge gave 3.49 ms against 3.79 ms. A new form of the light loop that holds one weight across pathLight returned the shader to register 115.

direct.frag is at register 112 and has three registers of room. direct_key_fill.frag has none, so any change to a file that it includes can cross the step again.

The work:

1. Make room: find which values the shader holds at its peak, and free two or three registers, so that an unrelated change does not cross the step.
2. Guard the count. The offline measure of t2-vsqh compiles the SPIR-V for the M1's GPU with Apple's tools and reads the highest register with the applegpu decoder. Its scripts are in docs/research/light-pairs/native/ (registers.sh, variant.sh), with the scratch paths of that work, and docs/research/light-pairs/pair/notes.md, 'Offline native shader evidence', names the decoder. Decide whether it becomes a tool in tools/ with a check that a developer or the hook runs when a file that direct_key_fill.frag includes changes.
3. If no guard is possible, name the limit in a comment at the top of each file that the shader includes.

Acceptance: a change that raises direct_key_fill.frag above register 115 is reported before the merge, or the shader has room of at least two registers and the document says how to measure it.
