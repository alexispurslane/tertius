# xctrace does not end after the run in about one trace of five, and gpu-trace.sh then loses the trace

In t2-mlvy, 6 of 41 traces were lost (docs/research/light-pairs/measurements.md, the rows 'trace lost'). The run ends and prints its own frame span, xctrace prints 'Target app exited, ending recording...' and then never ends. A watchdog of the ticket sent INT after three minutes, and xctrace exited with 130 without a usable trace. The lost runs have nothing in common that the ticket found: variants x2, x9 and x13, the floor in the hall, and main under the stars scene. Find out where xctrace waits, or make gpu-trace.sh end it in a way that keeps the recording, or retry the run once.


## Notes

**2026-11-12T09:31:14Z**

2026-11-12 orchestrator: copied to main from branch t2-mlvy-tile-light-budget, priority raised to 1. At 03:24 a Metal System Trace of ter-nstr's timing take (trace/stock-branch) hung the same way and held the EXCLUSIVE GPU lock for 66 minutes while six other agents' takes waited. Sending SIGINT to xctrace (kill -INT <xctrace pid>) ended it cleanly: it wrote a valid trace and the take finished. So the fix is cheap: the trace launcher sends xctrace SIGINT when the launched app has exited (or after a time limit), instead of waiting for xctrace to notice.
