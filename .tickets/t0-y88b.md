# A still frame's reprojected place lies a float rounding off its texel's center, so its three other taps take weights of 1e-7 to 1e-3 and pull a history length a hair under 8

Found by step 1 of t0-xwdz (docs/research/t0-xwdz-reflection-tail/README.md). In a still frame a pixel's place in the last frame's image should be the center of its own texel, so reprojectionOf (signals.glsl) should give its own tap the weight 1 and the three other taps the weight 0. Measured in the still cabin at draw 5, 1280 x 720: 430,000 of 904,500 surface pixels give the three other taps a weight share from 1e-7 to 1e-3, 473,000 give exactly 0, and none lies between 1e-3 and 1e-2. The size fits the float spacing of a normalized place near 1 (6e-8) times the image's width: the place is stored as a share of the image and converted back by texelPlace = place * frame.previousViewport.xy - 0.5. (The last step is the lead's inference from the size, not logged.)

What it costs: next to a neighbor whose history a real anti-lag cut shortened, the tiny weights pull the reprojected length less than 1e-3 under its own texel's. The 32-bit history keeps 7.9999, and the ray plan's test historyLengthOf >= FullRateFrames fails. At draw 7 the cabin holds 82,866 such lengths and the bay 104,037; 97.8 % and 98.7 % of them come from these weights. They give 37.4 % (cabin, 66,035) and 34.1 % (bay, 76,773) of draw 8's own reflection rays, which buy nothing: a history of 7.9999 draws is as good as one of 8.

The same rounding already has one local workaround: LeastFootprintWeight in screen_trace_plan.comp, whose comment names 'the rounding of a still frame's pixel place'. So the class of this bug is every reader of a reprojection's weights, and the fix belongs in the one place that makes the weights.

## Design

One choke point: reprojectionOf(vec2 place, uint bits) in signals.glsl makes every reprojection's taps and weights. The fix goes there and nowhere else, so that the accumulation, the plan's length and the light record all read an exact still frame.

The rule: a place within StillPlaceTexels of a texel's center, along an axis, reads that texel's center along that axis.

    // The largest distance in texels, along an axis, between a place and a texel's center at which the place counts as
    // that center. The rounding of a still frame's place is up to about 1e-3 texel at an image 8,000 pixels wide, and a
    // motion of 1 / 256 texel per draw leaves the history at most 1 / 8 texel behind over 32 draws.
    const float StillPlaceTexels = 1.0 / 256.0;

    vec2 texelPlace = place * frame.previousViewport.xy - 0.5;
    vec2 center = round(texelPlace);
    texelPlace = mix(texelPlace, center, lessThan(abs(texelPlace - center), vec2(StillPlaceTexels)));

No tolerance goes into the plan's test (historyLengthOf >= FullRateFrames stays exact): a tolerance there would hide the deficit and leave the light record and the accumulation reading the neighbors. The 32-bit store and the blend are not at fault (0 events each).

LeastFootprintWeight stays unless its tests show that the still frame was its only reason. Its comment loses the sentence about the still frame's rounding if the snap makes it untrue. The lead reports which.

Rejected: places in texel units end to end (exact by construction, but the place is a share of the image in every reader, so the change is wide), and a rounded length in the plan's test (it fixes one reader).

## Acceptance Criteria

1. Tests first, red on today's code: in a still frame, a reprojection's own tap has the weight 1 exactly and the others 0, at image widths of 1280 and 3840; a place 1/64 texel off a center keeps its bilinear weights; a still pixel next to a neighbor with a shorter history holds a length of exactly 8 after 8 draws. 2. The frozen cabin and bay of t0-xwdz's log: no length in [7.999, 8) at draw 7 from a still place, and draw 8's own reflection rays fall by about a third (cabin 176,403, bay 224,864 before). 3. Frozen stills of the cabin and the bay at 1:1 show no visible change against main. 4. Checked and Release suites, one configuration at a time; the mutants of the changed shader lines by tools/mutate.py; one review.
