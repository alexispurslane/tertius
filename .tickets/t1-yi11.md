## Terms

- **Blob**: a soft blotch of the diffuse signal, the band from 3 to 36 px of a still's deviation from the late mean of its own take, as a standard deviation over a region in units of main's full mean.
- **Cap**: the largest length of the slow diffuse history, in draws (SignalParameters.Diffuse, Cap and SmoothCap, 32 on main).
- **Region evidence**: the action of the region drift test (region_drift.glsl): where the test fires for a region's block, the diffuse history's length is cut to at most FullRateFrames, 8 draws.
- **Frozen bay**: bay-sunbeam at 2/3 and -4.089 stops, a frozen world, in which nothing changes. Every firing there is a false one.

- **The effect** is a change of the region's light. Today's test reads only the effect: it compares the mean of the last 18 draws with the mean of the 18 before.

### Design E: read the effect, with the signed rank test

The same question as today, "did the region's light move between the two windows", asked of signs and ranks. The answer's action stays: the cut to `FullRateFrames`.

```glsl
// A sketch of regionTestOf, in place of the comparison of the two means:
float d = lightBack(i) - lightBack(i + RegionWindowDraws);
...
return max(rankSumAbove, rankSumBelow) >= RankBar;
```

where

- `lightBack(i)` = the region's light of the draw i draws back, this draw for i = 0, from the ring.
- `d` = the difference between the region's light of the draw i draws back and of the draw 18 draws before that one, for i = 0 to 17. The two draws have the same jitter phase, so the jitter's cycle cancels in each difference.
- `rankSumAbove` = the sum of the ranks of the differences above 0, where the rank of a difference is its place from 1 to 18 in the order of the sizes |d|. `rankSumBelow` = the same for the differences below 0. A difference of exactly 0 goes into neither sum.
- `RankBar` = 166 of 171. On noise of any distribution, 20 of the 2^18 sign patterns reach it: one false answer in about 13,000 tested draws of an anchor.

What the level rests on (decision Y4 wants a reason): a block lies in the regions of 4 to 9 anchors, and a cut grows back in about 120 draws at a cap of 128. A false rate of 7.6e-5 then leaves about 4 % of the blocks short at any moment (4 x 120 x 7.6e-5), which the model confirms (3.1 %). The next levels that 18 differences offer are 5.3e-5 and 1.1e-4.

Why ranks and no higher bar on today's statistic: a rare bright draw moves a mean, and it makes the older window's spread a poor measure (the still cabin answers yes at 8 standard deviations in 0.017 % of its tested draws). A rank cannot grow with the draw's size, and its false rate does not depend on the noise's distribution. At a like false rate the rank test answers earlier in rect-bay than today's test at 5 standard deviations (off 19.8 against 28.2 %).

- State added: none. The ring keeps its 36 draws. The test is one function of `region_drift.glsl`.
- In the frozen bay: not column d. About 3 % of the blocks are short at any moment, in soft patches of a region's size that come and go: about 1 to 2 false answers a second on the screen (240 anchors, 60 draws a second, 0.007 to 0.015 % measured).
- At a light change: later than column b. The test needs most of its 18 differences on the new side, so it answers about 9 to 12 draws after today's test (median, replay). The model puts it near column c on rect-bay and later than c on the cabin switch.
- What it cannot see: a change that is too slow or too small to order 18 draws. No statistic on the effect removes this. A history of n draws is at its best length where the drift over n draws is as large as the noise of a mean of n draws, a statistic of about 1. A test that is to be silent in a frozen world needs 4 to 5. So an effect test alone always holds some histories longer than is best, or cuts some falsely.

### Design C: the cause says whether, the effect says how much

The model: a region's light changes only after a cause. The cause is known without noise where a ray brings it. So the question "did the light of this region really change" has two parts with two sources. Whether anything could have changed it: the region's own rays, which are exact. How much it moved: today's statistic, which sees every change of size. Today's test may answer yes only while one of the region's taps brought a cause in the recent past. This is decision Y3's model, used as one condition: the ray gives exact knowledge, the statistic sees every change, and both end in one evidence value with one reader.

```glsl
// A sketch. regionTapOf: one more component, 1 where the counted tap's sample brings a cause.
bool cause = evidenceOfSample(imageLoad(sampleAge, pixel).r) != NoEvidence;

// updateRegionAnchor, invocation 0:
float causeAge = !valid || sums.w > 0.0 ? 0.0 : min(causeAgeBefore + 1.0, parameters.cap);
bool moved = causeAge < parameters.cap && regionTestOf(now);
imageStore(regionRing, ivec3(texel, int(parameters.regionSlot)), vec4(now.light, now.validDraws, moved ? 1.0 : 0.0, causeAge));
```

where

- `evidenceOfSample(...)` = the evidence level of one sample from its sample age: light evidence for a recent light code, motion evidence for the motion bit, else none. It exists today for the block's own samples.
- `sums.w` = the count of the region's counted taps whose sample brings a cause in this draw (at most 289).
- `valid` = whether this draw is a valid draw of the anchor: at least half of its taps count. A draw that is not valid is a cause itself: the view moved.
- `causeAge` = the draws since a tap of the region last brought a cause, in draws, at most the cap. It lives in the ring's free channel `a`. 0 after a reset, so a start counts as a cause.
- `causeAgeBefore` = `causeAge` of the draw before, 0 where this draw does not continue it.
- `parameters.cap` = the diffuse history's cap, in draws. It is the gate's length for a reason: a history that a cause cut needs up to the cap's draws to grow back, and the light of the later bounces settles in that time.
- `moved` = the anchor's answer, which the blocks read one draw later, as today.

- State added: one number per anchor, `causeAge`, in a channel that the ring already has. Owner: the anchor's workgroup, the ring's only writer. No reader outside `region_drift.glsl` changes.
- In the frozen bay: column d by construction, at any cap, once the cap's draws have passed since the start. This rests on one measured fact: light evidence writes no short history in the frozen bay (t1-yi11's table), so no tap brings a cause there. The probe counts it.
- At a light change: today's test, so column b, as long as a ray of the region sees the cause. The cabin switch's whole settle (81 draws) lies inside the gate.
- It is not the rejected spread of ray evidence (decision Y2, the probe "spread 4"). That probe gave a block the evidence of the rays nearby, so a few lucky rays cut whole patches. Here a ray's cause cuts nothing. It only lets the region's statistic speak, and the statistic still decides by the light of all 289 taps.
- What it cannot see: a change whose cause no ray of the region hits. Those are section 9's causes without a signal: a region lit only by the bounce of a changed surface that it does not see (row 9), the sky's or the environment's light (row 11), a sender off the screen (the ray's fallback light), and a base color (row 14). Today the statistic follows them. Under the gate they follow at 1 / cap per draw.
- Where it gains nothing: a scene in which a cause never ends in view, such as a live bay whose sun patch creeps. There the gate stays open and the false cuts are column b's. That is no worse than main.

### The forks

1. **What says whether the light changed**: the effect alone at a strict level (E), or the rays' causes (C).
2. **In C, what sets the size of the cut while a cause is in view**: today's test with its cut to 8 (column b's films, known), the repaired cap (column c's), or the share of the region's rays that bring a cause, with no statistic (the orchestrator's proportional form). The third needs an assumption about how much a changed sender's light changed, because a light code holds an age and no size. It also replaces the statistic that Alexis chose on 2026-11-14 (decision Y1) and comes close to the ray evidence that decision Y2 rejected.
3. **In C, the causes that no ray shows**: accept them as lag at the cap, or keep design E's test beside the gate as a second line, at the price of E's false cuts in a frozen world.
4. **In C, how long a cause keeps the gate open**: the cap (the histories' time to grow back), or the ring's 36 draws.

### My recommendation

Design C, with today's test inside the gate, the gate's length at the cap, and the unseen causes accepted as lag for now.

- It is the only one of the two that makes the frozen world's false cut impossible, and it needs no level to tune. Design E moves along the trade that the repaired cap already moved along.
- It changes least: on every light change that a ray sees, the code that runs is today's, whose films pass at a cap of 128 (column b).
- It keeps one model. The exact knowledge and the statistic are one answer, as decision Y3 says, and no second mechanism appears beside the first.
- The picture that would show its miss: a room that a lamp lights only by bounce, around a corner from every surface that the lamp lights directly, at the lamp's switch. With the gate that room trails by up to the cap's draws. If a scene of the game shows it, design E's test as a second line is the measured next step (fork 3). No scene of the survey has such a room today, as far as I know.
- The proportional form (fork 2, third option) is the one that would help a live scene with a creeping sun patch. I leave it out until the probe's count shows how many rays bring a cause there, and until a film of such a scene shows column b's blobs.
