# Region evidence's false firings undo a longer diffuse history: with them off, a cap of 128 cuts the frozen bay's diffuse blobs 2 to 3.4 times

A finding of the guide census (t1-nyjl), 2026-11-23, measured by the surface share's lead. It is the one writer that the orchestrator asked for as the next ticket for the blobs. No work is ordered: the rules of the temporal stack are the Design X line's ground, and the orchestrator decides who takes it and in what form. It adds to t0-qvm1 (closed on 2026-11-23 with no visible gain at a cap of 32) the two facts that explain that outcome.

## Terms

- **Blob**: a soft blotch of the diffuse signal, the band from 3 to 36 px of a still's deviation from the late mean of its own take, as a standard deviation over a region in units of main's full mean.
- **Cap**: the largest length of the slow diffuse history, in draws (SignalParameters.Diffuse, Cap and SmoothCap, 32 on main).
- **Region evidence**: the action of the region drift test (region_drift.glsl): where the test fires for a region's block, the diffuse history's length is cut to at most FullRateFrames, 8 draws.
- **Frozen bay**: bay-sunbeam at 2/3 and -4.089 stops, a frozen world, in which nothing changes. Every firing there is a false one.

## The two facts that t0-qvm1's takes could not see

1. At a cap of 32 the false firings cost little, because a history of 16 to 24 draws is not far from one of 32. They cost at a longer cap, where they undo most of the cap's gain. Neither lever shows alone: a cap of 128 with region evidence on leaves the blobs as they are, and region evidence off at a cap of 32 was the region cap's small gain.
2. In the full picture the bay's right wall and the wall beside the sunlit stripe are covered by the specular signal's grain (t1-nyjl, ter-fe63), so a change of the diffuse signal could not show there at 1:1.

## What a design has to weigh (not designed)

- Region evidence exists for a real change of the light. With it off, or with a longer cap, a lamp switch and rect-bay's moving sunbeam settle later. The region cap's takes hold those costs for the cap of 32: the fair cabin switch settled 12 draws later, and rect-bay's left wall stayed lighter while the sunbeam left.
- The candidates that the records already name: no region evidence for the diffuse signal; the count gate of t0-qvm1's replay (the cut only from the third tested draw in a row above the bar); a test that does not fire on the scatter of rare bright draws (the title of t0-qvm1). And beside any of them, a longer cap.
- The measure of success is a picture: the frozen bay's floor and back wall at 1:1 in the diffuse signal and in the full picture, and the films of a light change beside main.
