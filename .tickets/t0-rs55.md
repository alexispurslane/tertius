# The fix of short histories reads every pixel of each tapped block, so the first draws after a reset use all the rays that were traced

Found by the catch-up ray experiment t0-gumm on 2026-11-22 (docs/research/t0-gumm-catch-up-spread, "The 36-tap fix"), as a scratch variant that the orchestrator named. Not built. The build comes after the merge of Design X (t0-iido).

## The fault

After a reset of the history (a start or a camera cut), and for newly seen pixels, a history is shorter than HistoryFixFrames (4 draws). The spatial dispatch then replaces the pixel's slow history with the length-weighted mean of 3 x 3 taps that lie `spacing` pixels apart: 4 pixels at a length of 1, 2 at a length of 2, 1 at a length of 3 (accumulate_spatial.comp, step 3). The screen tracer shares one ray per block of 2 x 2 pixels, and gives each other pixel of a young block a ray of its own. So while the spacing is 2 or more, each tap lies in another block, and the fix reads one pixel of four that hold independent samples. In the first draws it builds the picture from 9 samples per pixel while 36 were traced around the same taps. Three quarters of the rays of the dearest draws do not reach the fix.

## Measured on the scratch form (bay-sunbeam's start, Mac, constant scale 2/3)

- Grain of draws 1 to 4: 3.94 % with today's fix, 2.71 % with the wide fix, 31 % less; the bay's spread between runs is 0.08.
- Per draw: 12.3 to 6.2 at draw 0, 6.0 to 2.3 at draw 1, 3.4 to 2.8 at draw 2; 0.1 apart by draw 8 and equal from draw 9.
- Boil of draws 1 to 4: 2.289 % against 2.254 %, 0.035 higher with the wide fix (the spread is 0.02): draw 1 boils more and draw 2 less.
- Narrow crop's grain of draws 1 to 4: 0.66 against 0.57.
- The control: with no own rays in draws 0 to 2 (the delayed shape) the wide fix changes nothing, grain 3.883 against 3.880. So the gain comes from the block mates' independent samples, as the model says.
- No shape of catch-up rays in t0-gumm's sweep made the first draws of a bounce-lit room better than today. This does, at no ray.

## Design

## The model

A ray reaches the picture only through a pool: a pixel takes one sample per draw whatever the plan does, and an own ray only makes that sample independent of its block mates (t0-gumm, the code finding). The fix of short histories is the first pool after a reset. It must read every sample that was traced for the blocks it taps. No new state and no new pass: the fix stays the one owner of a short history's replacement, and it reads the same scratch image that the temporal dispatch wrote in this draw.

## What changes

accumulate_spatial.comp, step 3. While the fix's spacing is 2 or more, each of its 3 x 3 taps reads every pixel of the tap's 2 x 2 block, the own block's other three pixels too, each with its length as the weight and under today's surface test. At a spacing of 1 the taps already lie in neighbouring pixels, and the fix stays today's 3 x 3. The scratch form, as it ran:

    int side = spacing >= 2 ? 2 : 1;
    for (int y = -1; y <= 1; y++)
        for (int x = -1; x <= 1; x++)
            for (int by = 0; by < side; by++)
                for (int bx = 0; bx < side; bx++)
                {
                    ivec2 tap = pixel + spacing * ivec2(x, y);
                    ivec2 neighbor = side == 2 ? (tap & ivec2(~1)) + ivec2(bx, by) : tap;
                    if (neighbor == pixel || !insideImages(neighbor) || !sameSurface(key, surfaceKeyAt(neighbor)))
                        continue;
                    ...
                }

where pixel is the pixel whose short history the fix replaces, spacing is the distance between the taps in pixels, tap is the pixel that today's fix reads, tap & ivec2(~1) is the first pixel of the tap's block (the tracer's blocks start at even pixels), and neighbor is one of the block's up to four pixels. The lead that builds it may give the inner part a named function (for example the sum over one block) if that keeps the loop readable; the block's size must come from the same place as the tracer's block (screen_trace.glsl), not from a second constant.

## Decided (the orchestrator can reverse)

- Direct reads of the block's pixels, not a new half-size image of block means. A block-mean image would be a second store of the scratch image's light, written by the temporal dispatch for a reader that needs it in at most 2 draws per young pixel, and it could not apply the pixel's own surface test to each block mate.
- Both signals take it, because both share the fix and both trace own rays in young blocks. The reflections are not measured yet; if they get worse, the ticket says so and limits the change to the bounce light with the reason.
- No switch in production. The probe variable of the scratch form goes away.

## Open, to measure in the build

- The GPU cost. After a reset every pixel is young, so up to 27 more reads of the scratch image and of the surface key per pixel fall into draws 1 and 2, inside the cost peak of the catch-up rays (ter-4iic) and inside the known dip of the render scale (ter-bcwr). Measure the GPU span of draws 0 to 3 after a start and a cut on the Mac and the card's render scale log, against today's.
- A film of motion with newly seen pixels (the cabin walk and a turn): the fix then reads four pixels around each tap in place of one, at the same reach. Check the edges of newly seen surfaces.
- The boil of draw 1, which rose in the bay.

## Out of scope

The shape of the catch-up rays (today's cliff stays), the known dip of the render scale (ter-bcwr), the region test (t0-qvm1), the fix at a spacing of 1.

## Acceptance Criteria

Tests first, red on today's fix: a short history whose tapped blocks hold four different values takes the length-weighted mean of every pixel of those blocks that shows its surface; a block mate on another surface is left out; at a spacing of 1 the fix is today's. In bay-sunbeam's start at the constant scale 2/3 the grain of draws 1 to 4 falls by about a third against today's fix and no measure of the sweep's table gets worse by more than the spread, the boil of draw 1 named. The cabin's start and cut, the lamp switch and the per-scene stills are no worse. The cabin walk's film shows no new fault at newly seen edges. The GPU span of draws 0 to 3 after a start and a cut and the card's render scale log are reported against today's. The cost of draws 1 and 2 on the card under a start and a cut fits inside the known dip of the render scale (ter-bcwr, 8 draws long), and the ticket's record says so with the numbers. The build names every reader of the screen tracer's sample images and of the scratch image and says for each whether it reads one pixel per block or all four while own rays exist; a reader with the same pooling fault gets its own row in this ticket and is not fixed on spec.
