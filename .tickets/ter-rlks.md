# Earth system: the Moon, the demo's date, and a scene that shows the Sun, Earth, the Moon and the station

The user asked on 2026-10-20 for the demo's world to have the Sun at its true size, Earth at its true size and distance on a Kepler orbit round the Sun, Earth's surface frame turning with the sidereal day and ending 100 km up, inside a larger Earth orbit frame that does not turn and holds the station and a Moon, which orbits Earth further out on a Kepler orbit and does not turn. DemoScenes.SunAndEarth (the orbit scene) already builds the Sun, Earth's orbit, the frame earth and the turning earth-surface. This ticket adds the Moon and a date to that builder, and a new scene with views and screenshots. The demo's own scene moves into the system in the next ticket.
