# The acceptance takes of the back-face layer: stills beside the reference, frame cost on both machines, and a Linux run

The last step of the design `docs/plans/back-face-depth-layer.md`. Read its section "The acceptance" first, then the record `docs/research/back-face-depth-layer/README.md`. This ticket is an experiment: it measures the finished branch and adds no engine code.

The branch holds the finished layer after ticket `t1-7b4x`. This ticket compares it with main and with the path-traced reference of stage 2. Each result goes into one document, and the line orchestrator reports from that document.

## Settled decisions

- Main's side of each comparison is main at the time of the take. A fix of the history store (`t8-69v7`) changes main's brightness by 2 to 6 %. The fix of the sun's shadow leak (`t8-rjno`) darkens `rect-lamps` on main too. The line orchestrator merges main into the branch before this ticket starts.
- The scenes of the frame cost are the four of stage 1b: the deck, the cabin, `rect-lamps` and the crowd. The method is stage 1b's: interleaved rounds in a balanced order, a fixed exposure and a recorded GPU clock. Each scene has one exclusive hold of the GPU lock, and a hold lasts at most about 20 minutes. Each side has at least five kept rounds.
- A still is frame 239 of a film, because a capture resets the histories.
- The census needs a counter in the shader. It stays a patch and a scratch commit, as in stage 1b. The branch gets documents, scripts and small tools only.
- If lavapipe cannot run a test, name the test and the reason. Do not skip it silently.
- The cloud budget of this ticket is $1.00 by the job log's estimates.

## Deliverables

- `docs/research/back-face-depth-layer/acceptance.md`: one section per point of the table, with the numbers, the commands and what was not measured.
- Three to six crops beside the reference, each with a one-line caption, as PNG files beside the document.
- The frame cost per scene and machine as a table: main, the branch, the difference and the spread.

## Acceptance

- Each point of the table has a measured result or a named reason why it has none.
- A point that fails is stated as a failure, with its picture or its number.

## Out of scope

Changes to the engine. A defect that the takes find becomes a bug ticket.
