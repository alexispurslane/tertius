# At every start the render scale falls to about 0.77 within 9 draws and needs about 100 draws to climb back, because the first 8 draws trace a ray at every pixel

Evidence of t0-3vwx (the card's time and per-pass logs, /private/tmp/t0-gbgj/cloud5/times/out/times, job 89e20008, cloudvideo.sh times with scratch-pass-log.patch; analysis tools/timeslog.py of docs/research/t0-3vwx-dynamic-scale-films). Runs without pictures under FrameTimeTarget(16.67 ms, 0.5, 1) on the RTX 2000 Ada, main ac5f23915 (M5) and Design X merged with it (X5), the walk cabin-walk and the switch cabin, two runs each.

On every run that is not the first run of its binary (walk r2, switch r1 and r2, on both codes), draws 0 and 1 at 1920 x 1080 cost 26.3 to 26.5 ms of GPU span, of which 23.1 to 24.5 ms at the render size: diffuse_rays 5.77 ms, filter_indirect_diffuse 5.35 ms, reflection_rays 4.85 ms. diffuse_rays costs about 2.7 ms per million render pixels on draws 0 to 7 and 0.72 from draw 9 (3.40 ms at draw 7, 1504 x 840; 1.32 ms at draw 8, 1488 x 832), about a quarter; filter_indirect_diffuse falls by about half at draw 3. Chart: docs/research/t0-3vwx-dynamic-scale-films/pics/start-scale-span.png. This fits ter-4iic: every history is under FullRateFrames = 8 draws and keeps its own rays. The controller reads draws 0 to 7 as the scene's cost: the scale falls to 0.88 at draw 3 and 0.771 at draw 9, and climbs back to 0.92 by draw 40 and 0.99 by draw 100. The camera does not move in these draws (the still's 33 draws and the start of the stand film). Main and X5 behave the same.

The two ray passes are at the render size, so the fall is the controller's correct answer to a real but passing cost. The question for the back-face line: does a game window show the same fall after a start and after each camera cut, and should the young histories' rays or the controller's reading of the first 8 draws change (ter-4iic holds the cost; this ticket holds its effect on the render scale).

## Acceptance Criteria

A run under the dynamic scale shows the scale after a start and after a camera cut within 0.05 of the scale that it settles at, or the record says why the fall is right, with the frame time that a window shows in those draws.
