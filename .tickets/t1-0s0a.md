# The depth pyramid stores a front w and a back w at every level, with the image unchanged

Step 1 of the design `docs/plans/back-face-depth-layer.md`. Read that document first: its terms, "The model", decision 2, "The interfaces" and "The order of the work". This ticket moves the slab's back into the depth pyramid and changes no pixel.

## System design

Today the walk computes a slab's back itself: it multiplies a texel's far bound by `1 / (1 + ThicknessShare)` at every step. After this ticket, the pyramid's build computes the back w once, and the walk reads it. The rule stays the 10 % rule, so every frame stays the same.

| State | Writer | Before | After |
|---|---|---|---|
| The finest level of the depth pyramid | `depth_pyramid_finest.comp` | R32F: the center w, 0 for sky | RG32F: R the center w, G the back w `centerW * backShare`. Sky holds (0, 0). |
| Levels 1 and above | `depth_pyramid_first.comp`, then the down and tail shaders | RG32F: (near w, far w) | RG32F: (near w, back w). Level 1 stores `farW * backShare` per pixel before the merge. The levels above merge as today. |
| The light pyramid | Its own shaders | R32F at the finest level | Unchanged |

`backShare` is `1.0 / (1.0 + ThicknessShare)`, multiplied as the walk does today. A divide rounds differently on most pixels (stage-1b/probe.md, "The settled details"). A multiply by a positive constant keeps the order, so the merge of level 1 gives the same value as today's multiply after the merge.

The invariant that the walk depends on: the slab of every cell holds the slab of every pixel under it.

## Signatures

GLSL, in `depth_pyramid.glsl`:

```glsl
// The w of the back of what is solid behind a surface at inverse depth `w`: the 10 % rule in this ticket.
float slabBack(float w);

// The slab of a pixel at the finest level: x the center w, y the back w, or (0, 0) for sky.
vec2 pixelSlab(ivec2 pixel);

// The bounds of a pixel for level 1: x its plane's near w, y the back w of its slab from its plane's far w, or the
// empty bounds for sky.
vec2 pixelSlabBounds(ivec2 pixel);
```

The walk, in `screen_walk.glsl`:

```glsl
cellBounds = level == 0 ? texelFetch(finest, ivec2(cell), 0).rg : texelFetch(bounds, ivec2(cell), level - 1).rg;
float near = cellBounds.x;
float back = cellBounds.y;
```

The signature of `walkScreen` does not change. The constant `backShare` leaves the walk.

C#: the copies follow the shaders. `PyramidImages` gives the depth pyramid's finest image the format RG32F, and the light pyramid's stays R32F. `CellBounds`, `DepthPyramid` and `ScreenRay.Walk` in `src/Tertius.Rendering/Shading` hold the back w where they hold the far bound today, and `ScreenRay.Walk` reads it with no multiply. The readers of the finest level through `VulkanRenderer.ReadDepthFinest` get both parts. Name the new members as the shader names them.

The other readers of the finest level read `.r` and do not change: three places in `screen_trace.glsl`.

## Settled decisions

- `ThicknessShare` and `thickness.glsl` stay in this ticket. Step 4 (`t1-7b4x`) removes them.
- The probe of stage 1b shows the whole change: `docs/research/back-face-depth-layer/stage-1b/probe-slab-in-pyramid.patch`, its mode `off`. Take what fits, and write the real code to STYLE.md. The throwaway `BackFacePass` on the branch stays as it is here.
- The depth pyramid's module lists its invariants and has one method that checks them, as CLAUDE.md's rules on contracts ask. The cell invariant above is one of them.
