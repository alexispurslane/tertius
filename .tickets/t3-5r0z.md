# The reflections fail on their own in the films docking-landing and bay-sunbeam

Found by the split by light signal of ter-6k3k (docs/plans/renderer-finish.md, "The split by light signal"). The reflections are 10 to 20 % of the light, but their error alone exceeds the blotch limit of 0.04: docking-landing 0.105 at the middle frame and 0.059 at the last, with a moving camera; bay-sunbeam 0.066 at the last frame, where the reflections trail the moving sunbeam by 62 % and 20 %. Guided diffuse rays and the diffuse history cut do not touch the reflections. Step 2 (ter-4oow, shared reflection rays by lobe fit) changes the reflection rays, so split both films again after it merges, then find the cause (reflection history under a moving camera or light, see tiunr-ax4c).

## Acceptance Criteria

The split of docking-landing and bay-sunbeam gives a reflection blotch of at most 0.04 at both sampled frames.


## Notes

**2026-11-22T07:58:43Z**

The region's drift test for the reflections was tried in t0-acxm and rejected (remedy lead, 2026-11-22; docs/research/t0-acxm-region-evidence/README.md). Measured on main after the shadow fix, in the bay film rect-bay: with the test for both signals, the last frame's specks were 1.07, against 0.27 with the bounce light alone and 0.89 on main. Level (+0.84 / +0.28 against +0.85 / +0.29) and lag error (0.150 both) did not change, so the reflections' test did not help the bay's trail. In still views the reflections' test fired falsely in 2 of 28 tested draws (about 7 %): their region light of one tap per block swings between 0.24 and 0.46 from draw to draw. The bounce light's fired in none. Cost: in the remedy's queues the reflections' test held about 0.08 ms of the deck's +0.091 ms; in the bay rounds, dropping it saved 0.026 ms on the deck (spread 0.106) and cost 0.223 ms in the film, because the reflections then kept their long histories. In the GPU trace of the deck, the reflections' accumulation pass rose by 0.049 ms with it. So a regional statistic of one tap per block is too noisy for the reflections.
