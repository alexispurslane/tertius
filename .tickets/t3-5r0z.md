# The reflections fail on their own in the films docking-landing and bay-sunbeam

Found by the split by light signal of ter-6k3k (docs/plans/renderer-finish.md, "The split by light signal"). The reflections are 10 to 20 % of the light, but their error alone exceeds the blotch limit of 0.04: docking-landing 0.105 at the middle frame and 0.059 at the last, with a moving camera; bay-sunbeam 0.066 at the last frame, where the reflections trail the moving sunbeam by 62 % and 20 %. Guided diffuse rays and the diffuse history cut do not touch the reflections. Step 2 (ter-4oow, shared reflection rays by lobe fit) changes the reflection rays, so split both films again after it merges, then find the cause (reflection history under a moving camera or light, see tiunr-ax4c).

## Acceptance Criteria

The split of docking-landing and bay-sunbeam gives a reflection blotch of at most 0.04 at both sampled frames.
