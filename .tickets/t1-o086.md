# One weld for a mesh's positions: the card numbering and the closure test answer 'which vertices are one point' by two rules

**2026-11-25 · Retired.** The back-face depth and screen-ray study retains the probes. A future geometry or ray-hit change needs a production shot whose incorrect hit can be traced.

Found by the review of the mesh closure ticket (t1-6ucc). StagedMesh.Of asks which vertices are one point twice. MeshCards.Number (src/Tertius.Rendering/Shading/MeshCards.cs) joins vertices with exactly equal positions. MeshClosure (src/Tertius.Rendering/Resources/MeshClosure.cs) welds positions within a tolerance of the largest coordinate times 2^-16. The two rules can disagree on one mesh, and a change of the weld must find both places. Stage 2 of the back-face layer found that exact equality splits the pole vertices of a UV sphere, so exact equality can also split a hair strip into two cards on float noise. That is not shown on a real asset yet. The design: one weld, for example MeshWeld.Of(ReadOnlySpan<Vector3> positions) that returns the welded vertex of each position, owned by one type. MeshCards.Number and MeshClosure.IsClosed both take its result, and StagedMesh.Of computes it once. Risk: a card's number feeds the hair's shading, so a changed number can change a hair image. Check the hair's reference images, and review each one that changes.
