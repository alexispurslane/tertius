# The guide holds no light for what a reflection ray reads: on rect-lamps' floor 54 % of the reflection's light comes from lamps that the guide cannot aim at

**2026-11-25 · Retired.** Preserve the temporal lighting measurements. Choose any change to sampling, weighting or history only after isolating the source of the pattern on the new deck (ter-xzj7).

Written on the orchestrator's word of 2026-11-23, with the numbers of the replay of ter-fe63. No work is ordered. It is one of the two root causes that the guided mixture for reflection rays (ter-fe63) does not remove; the other is t1-cyhy.

## Terms

- **Guide light** of a pixel: the value by which the guided draw chooses the pixel as its target. Today it is the luminance of the pixel's sent diffuse light (sentDiffuseLight, the finest level of the light pyramid).
- **Hit light** of a kind: what a ray of that kind reads at the pixel that it hits (the layer of the image hitLight for the kind, written by the plan from leavingLightOf).
- **Guided share** s: the share of a kind's rays that take the guided draw.

## What happens

The guide was made for the diffuse kind, and its guide light is what a diffuse ray reads. A reflection ray reads the hit light's reflection layer, which is another light. A lamp's entity holds a light, so its glow sends nothing in the sent diffuse light (glowAt), because the direct pass already counts that light for a diffuse receiver. But a reflection ray that hits the lamp reads its glow: that is the lamp's picture in a glossy floor. So the guide gives no light to the senders that carry a glossy floor's reflection.

## The numbers (ter-fe63's replay, 2026-11-23, rect-lamps' glossy floor, roughness 0.38)

- 54 % of the second moment of the floor's reflection samples comes from hits on pixels to which the guide gives no light: the lamps.
- A guided draw's sample brings 0.04 of the pixel's mean there. On the nose's ceiling it brings 1.37 of the mean, and on the bay's right wall 1.61.
- So the mixture is only a tax on that floor. The relative variance of one reflection sample goes from 0.391 to 0.834 at s = 0.25 with the coin, and to 0.525 with the exact count of ter-fe63, which is the bound 1 / (1 - s).
- The mirror plate (roughness 0.05) is a different case: its reflection light comes only from misses, which no guide of screen pixels can aim at.

## What a build would have to decide (not designed)

The guide light of the reflection kind must hold what a reflection ray reads: the luminance of the hit light's reflection layer, or the sent diffuse light plus the glow that the diffuse kind leaves out. Two shapes to design twice when the ticket is taken: a second pyramid for the reflection kind, built by the same passes from another finest level; or one pyramid with two channels, one guide light per kind, which keeps the count of dispatches and doubles the texels' size. Either one adds cost to every frame, also where no glossy surface is in view, so the design starts from what the pyramid's passes cost today (te-r7nt holds the numbers of the pyramids' dispatches) and from which views gain: the test is rect-lamps' floor turning from a tax of 1.34 times into a gain.

## Out of scope

The guided draws that do not arrive (t1-cyhy). A guide for misses. The diffuse kind's guide light.
