# Live world design for a turning Earth: the ramp's direction at each pass, and the landing into the turning frame

Earth's surface turns once per sidereal day, so the launch ramp's direction turns by about 23 degrees between two passes of the station. Only the cinematic's first departure finds the ramp in the station's plane. Update docs/plans/live-world.md: a ramp on a turntable, or an ascent that turns into the station's plane, with the reason; the landing pad and the tow in the turning earth-surface; the way down entering it through the top of its bubble; and the times of the starts.
