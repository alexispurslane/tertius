# The shader's tube light lies 2 to 3 % above the C# tube where a capsule end lights the surface, on the Mac and on NVIDIA

TubeLightRenderTests measures the square's center in the HDR target against a glow square at the C# answer (diagnostic of 2026-11-13, t8-sjli). Ratios, Mac and RTX 2000 Ada: standing-on-the-middle 1.0266 and 1.0285, slanted-through-the-square 1.0206 and 1.0230, across-the-middle 0.9984 and 1.0005. The Mac's values are bit-identical before and after ter-8esk. The two cases above 2 % take most of their light from a capsule end near the square; across-the-middle has none. A short read found no cause: MaterialSample.TubeTerms and TubeView copy tubeResponse and tubeView of lighting.glsl term for term, the normalization 4R·halfLength + πR² equals the test's 2RL + πR², the reach window is about 1 there, the distance target is R32F, and the glow squares land within 0.02 % of the C# value. Next: evaluate the shader's tubeView and falloff in float at the center's exact position (a C# float copy, or a debug write of the terms) against the C# doubles, term by term: the side line, each half sphere, and the window. The two tube cases carry a band of 3 % until this closes.
