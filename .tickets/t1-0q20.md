# Skip an environment face's depth ranges that hold no draws: an empty face costs 0.28 ms on the Mac and 0.04 with the skip

**2026-11-25 · Retired.** This optimization was scoped to an earlier GPU workload. Reopen against a named film segment if its pass dominates a repeatable GPU timing there.

Written by the line orchestrator of the back-face line on 2026-11-23, from the split of the environment face's cost (t1-6tn2, docs/research/environment-face-cost/README.md, section 10.6, 'Skipping the empty depth ranges'). No build is ordered: today's deck frame gains nothing from it.

## What happens

Each face begins three depth-range renderings and one light rendering with five barrier flushes, also when a range has no draws. On MoltenVK each is a Metal encoder of its own. So a face that draws nothing still costs 0.28 to 0.31 ms on the Mac by the span.

## The numbers of the probe (the faces are bit-identical with and without the skip)

| Face | Span without / with the skip, ms | Passes without / with, ms |
|---|---|---|
| The empty face of map 0, at 16 | 0.280 / 0.037 | 0.097 / 0.049 |
| The empty face of map 0, at 32 | 0.286 / 0.028 | 0.116 / 0.053 |
| The lean face (own objects, no prefilter), at 16 | 0.299 / 0.097 | 0.160 / 0.106 |
| The lean face, at 32 | 0.411 / 0.175 | 0.269 / 0.209 |
| Today's face of the deck at 128 | 0.738 / 0.732 | 0.626 / 0.620 |

Loads at the GPU lock: 3.3 to 7.8.

## Who gains

A map with a depth range that holds no draws: a root map that holds only far objects, or an interior whose near range is empty. Today's deck face gains nothing, because all its ranges hold draws. The lean field face of the grid of capture points gained most (216 faces in 60 draws at 0.35 ms per draw in place of 1.11), but the user dropped that grid (ter-6fka).

## The change, if it is taken

The owner of a face's renderings does not begin a depth range without draws. The probe's patch is docs/research/environment-face-cost/probe.patch. Before any build: count, in the demo's scenes, how many faces per draw have an empty range, because that count times 0.08 ms is the whole gain. A test: a face with an empty range is bit-identical with and without the skip, on the Mac and on lavapipe (a rendering that is not begun must not leave an attachment in an undefined state that a later pass reads).

## Out of scope

The prefilter once per round (its own ticket).
