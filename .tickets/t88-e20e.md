# Without the Blue Danube art, the gray-box tram floats 2.9 m above the hull's floor and its nozzle 5.8 m behind its tail

Measured on ter-9wwx (2026-11-25): the fit sizes the shuttle's physics hull to the art, so Shuttle.Hull runs from (-6.92, -4.90, -15.02) to (6.92, 4.37, 14.07) in the shuttle's axes. Without the converted art, Shuttle.HullRender draws the gray-box tram, a box with half-extents (3, 2, 8) round the origin, and SpawnNozzle draws the nozzle at Shuttle.NozzleZ = Hull.Max.Z - 0.3 = 13.77 m.

Visible effect, in a checkout without the art: the tram's bottom lies 2.9 m above the plane of the hull's wheels, so the parked tram hangs 2.9 m over the pad and over the bay's deck. The nozzle hangs alone 5.8 m behind the tram's tail at z = 8 m. The physics is right: the invisible hull box stands on its wheels.

Wanted: a gray-box outside that fills the hull box, such as a drawn box of Shuttle.Hull round the tram, or the tram placed on the hull's floor with the nozzle at its tail. The scripts must not change, since only render rows change.
