# The distance cube: each environment map stores its inverse distances and their levels

Ticket 2 of ter-jw7g's design (docs/plans/environment-map-distances.md, 'Ticket 2' and 'The distance store'). An R16F cube per map with 1000 m / distance (the sky 0), written as a second attachment of the face pass, with solid-angle-weighted mips beside the radiance mips, binding 16, and the barriers. Acceptance: GPU tests 13 to 15 pass in Checked and Release; no reader reads it yet, so the captures of demo-surface, mirror-balls, pbr-spheres and cabin-light are byte for byte main's; validation shows no new message; the memory matches the design's 'Cost'.


## Notes

**2026-11-02T13:17:35Z**

2026-11-02, builder: the distance cube is built (docs/plans/environment-map-distances.md, ticket 2). EnvironmentMap holds Distance, an R16F cube array with the radiance map's face size and levels (log2(W) + 1), with a 2D view of level 0 of each layer, a level view per level and a cube array view. The face pass writes it as its second color attachment: environment_face.frag stores w = 1000 / max(length(inPosition), 0.05) in km⁻¹ (the radial distance from the eye, not the depth along the face's axis), and environment_sky.frag stores 0. Both attachments load and store alike in every range. The prefilter's step 1 runs environment_distance_downsample.comp beside each radiance dispatch of the same levels and map, under the same barriers (the flush count stays 5M + 2). The body of the downsample moved to environment_downsample.glsl, and the two stage files define its storage format: rgba16f or r16f. Barriers: a drawn face's distance level 0 is discarded after the cube's last readers (SampledFragmentCompute) and goes to a compute read; the mips go through General; the whole cube ends in SampledFragmentCompute. Binding 16 of the frame set holds the cube array view, partially bound, for the fragment and compute stages, with the sampler of binding 13, rewritten at each growth. EnvironmentMap refuses a device whose optimal tiling of R16_SFLOAT lacks color attachment, sampled, linear filter or storage (DistanceFormatProblem).

Tests, each red first. 13 AFaceStoresTheInverseDistanceOfTheSurfaceInEachTexel: red, every texel read 0 (nothing wrote the cube). 14 EachLevelOfTheDistanceCubeIsTheSolidAngleMeanOfItsChildren (32 and 128, three maps): red, level 0 read 0; after the face pass it failed on validation because levels 1 and up were never made. 15 ATexelHalfWallAndHalfSkyHoldsHalfTheWallsInverseDistance: red as 14. Its bound is 2e-3, two half-float roundings that Vulkan lets a device make toward zero; Metal measured 1.02e-3. The sampled-image count: TheSampledImagesOfALayoutAreTheMostThatOneStageSeesOutsideTheBindlessTextures was red at 15 before binding 16, now 16 for the scene layout and 18 for the impostor layout. The guards SampledImageProblem and DistanceFormatProblem: red against null stubs.

Captures: demo-surface, mirror-balls, pbr-spheres and cabin-light, 1920 x 1080, Release, headless, on the base 6c40880fa (an export of its files) and on the branch: all 15 PNG files equal byte for byte. Validation: cabin-light and demo-surface with VK_INSTANCE_LAYERS=VK_LAYER_KHRONOS_validation and VK_LAYER_VALIDATE_SYNC=1 on both builds: no message on either. Memory, the driver's requirement on the M1 Max: 294,912 B per map at W = 128 (262,140 B of texels) against 2,180,352 B of the other images, 13.5 %; 16,809,984 B at W = 1024 against 134,300,928 B, 12.5 %. The design estimated 256 KB and 12 % at 128 from the texels alone.

Settled choices: the design's sampled-image bound of 16 cannot hold for the impostor layout, which already saw 17 before this ticket and sees 18 now; the scene layout sees exactly 16. DescriptorLayouts counts each stage of each pipeline layout and refuses a device below the most, as it does for storage buffers. The face shader drops the design's redundant min: 1000 / max(d, 0.05) is already at most 20,000.

For ter-s6zr: binding 16, environmentDistanceMap, a textureCubeArray in environment.glsl, sampled with environmentSampler (binding 13, trilinear, clamped, every level). Cube i is map i, in map i's axes. Levels: log2(W) + 1, down to 1 x 1, 8 at W = 128; query them with textureQueryLevels. Level 0 holds the half float of 1000 / max(|p|, 0.05) in km⁻¹ for the fragment of the nearest range at that texel center, rounded as the device rounds a color attachment (to nearest or toward zero); the sky is exactly 0. Each later level is the solid-angle-weighted mean of its four children, rounded to a half again. After the prefilter the cube is in ShaderReadOnlyOptimal for the fragment and compute stages.

- S04 (floor 1 m): killed by the new test ASurfaceTenCentimetersFromTheEyeStoresItsTrueInverseDistance.
