# The environment face of every draw costs 0.64 to 0.72 ms on the Mac's deck, and 98 % of it does not depend on the face's size

Found by the face cost experiment (ter-yglw, docs/research/environment-face-cost/README.md, measured on main 65a24d189, after Design X). The experiment asked whether design P of the lighting review fits. Its numbers also describe today's frame: the engine draws one environment face in turn in every draw (EnvironmentMap.FacesFor), at a face size of 128.

## What today's frame pays

Face cost = the GPU span that one face's refresh adds to a draw: the passes 'environment faces' and 'environment prefilter'.

| Machine | Face cost at 128, today | Share of the 12 ms goal |
|---|---|---|
| Mac (Apple GPU, MoltenVK), deck, 1280 x 720 | 0.64 to 0.72 ms per draw (two measures, sections 4 and 7.1 of the record) | 5 to 6 % |
| RTX 2000 Ada | 0.178 ms per draw | |

The Mac's face costs 3.7 to 4.1 times the card's, more than the usual factor of 2.4 for a whole frame.

## Where it goes

Fitted through the sizes 128 and 32, 98 % of a face's cost at 32 does not shrink with the size (Mac 0.534 of 0.546 ms). The fixed part has three sources, from the code and the counts:

1. The face pass draws about 145,000 to 153,000 triangles per face at every size. Most are the Earth's sphere, Primitives.Sphere(EarthRadius, 256, 128), 65,536 triangles, which spans all three depth ranges of a face and so draws 3 times. The root map's faces draw nothing else. The ship's map draws the Earth through the windows.
2. The prefilter costs 0.30 ms per face on the Mac at 32 and 0.34 ms at 128. Its irradiance convolution (environment_irradiance.comp) writes a 16 x 16 face and reads the whole cube at 32 x 32 for each output texel: about 1.6 million reads per face.
3. The prefilter runs about 10 dispatches per face at 128 (7 at 32) with barrier flushes between its runs. On MoltenVK each compute dispatch behind a barrier is a Metal encoder of its own.

A cull of objects below one texel removes 0.04 % of the triangles and no measurable cost.

## What was measured about the levers

- Six faces in one pass cost less per face than one: on the Mac at 128, 0.517 ms against 0.640 ms (19 % less); at 32 with the cull, 0.35 to 0.38 ms against 0.52 ms (30 % less). On the card, 43 to 45 % less.
- Nothing else. A coarser Earth in a face, a far field that a face draws once, and a cheaper irradiance for a face are untested.
