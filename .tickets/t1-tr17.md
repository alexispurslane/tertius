# The count of camera cuts holds two causes: the eye's jump and the hand-off of the camera's frame

Found in the review of the white room's fix (t58-rcd6). No work on it now. This ticket is the place where a second signal lives if a later fault needs it.

## The fact

RenderSnapshotBuilder counts a cut when it builds a snapshot with a camera that it did not interpolate: a different camera, a gap in the camera's stamp, or the same camera in another frame. The test WhenTheCameraChangesFrameTheSnapshotCountsOneMoreCut pins the third case. So `RenderSnapshot.CameraCuts` rises for two different causes:
- **The eye jumped**: a different camera, or a camera that was not there in the last build. The eye's place changes in one step.
- **The camera's frame changed hands**: the same camera moves on smoothly, and only the frame whose axes the snapshot uses changes, for example at a doorway.

## The readers, and what each one needs

The renderer reads the count in one place, FrameHistory, and derives two things from it.

| Reader | What it reads | Which cause it needs |
|---|---|---|
| The histories' reset (FrameHistory.Resets, and through it every history image, the upscaler's history, the surface share's spread, the catch-up draws of the render scale) | a change of the count | Both. After a jump the last draw's images show another view. After a hand-off the last draw's positions are in other axes, which the history cannot read (the `Continued` record is null then). |
| The environment maps' full refresh (FrameHistory.Begin, RefreshesEveryFace, since t58-rcd6) | a change of the count that keeps the camera's frame | The jump only. A hand-off is answered by EnvironmentChain.Keeps (decision 9 of docs/plans/environment-maps-per-frame.md), and a full refresh at a doorway costs 6 to 9 ms on the Mac in a draw that no cut hides. |
| The impostors (bug t10-ogon) and the automatic exposure (bug t10-ck8w) | nothing today | To be stated in their tickets: each misses a cut. |

The list of every store that depends on the eye's place is in ARCHITECTURE.md beside the reset's text, since the merge of t58-rcd6.

## What t58-rcd6 does without a second signal

It tells the two causes apart with the comparison that the history already owns: a cut that keeps the camera's frame is a jump inside that frame. Its residual: a cut to a camera in another frame is a jump and a hand-off at once, and it goes through Keeps alone. The ancestor maps whose frame and frame below stay the same are kept, though the eye jumped inside the subtree that they leave out. Their error is that jump against the distance of what they show.

## When this ticket becomes work

When a picture shows the residual, or when a third reader needs one cause without the other. The design question then: the snapshot carries the cause (two counts, or a count and a flag), and the simulation's builder is the one place that knows it. That changes the render seam, so it needs its own design.
