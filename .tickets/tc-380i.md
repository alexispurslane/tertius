# The probe's kept PNGs of the survey's switch runs come out as a false-colour contour pattern under the options --bloom off --tone-curve hable

**2026-11-25 · Retired.** The old take, trace or survey workflow is off the critical path for the three film reproductions. Retain its failure report if that specific tool is used again.


Found by t1-yi11 on 2026-11-23. Not fixed and not explored.

## What happens

`tools/renderer-survey.py` renders a switch run with `extra=PROBE_OPTIONS` (`--bloom off --tone-curve hable`) and keeps the
PNGs of the film frames in `TERTIUS_PROBE_KEEP`. In t1-yi11's runs (build ya: 5144cbe9a with the survey probe and
t0-gumm's frozen patches, the cabin switch at a fixed 4.08 stops), every kept PNG showed a purple and green contour
pattern of concentric bands in place of the cabin. The probe's measures of the same runs are sound: main's settle
counts equal t0-qvm1's (81 / 71 after off, 54 / 34 after on).

The same switch run without the two options gives a normal picture of the cabin. Which of the two options causes it,
and whether it comes from the probe patch or from the renderer, is not measured.

## Why it matters

The survey keeps such PNGs as `images/switch/<case>-<k>.png`. A reader who opens them sees no cabin, and a lead who makes
a film of them (as t1-yi11 did first) films a pattern.

## How to see it

`python3 -I docs/research/t1-yi11-region-evidence/tools/yifilm.py ya sw-a switch` on the branch t1-yi11-region-evidence,
after `tools/builds.sh ya`, then open `/private/tmp/t1-yi11/films/sw-a/switch/f-000200.png`.
