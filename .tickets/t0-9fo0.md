# After the shadow fix: new takes of the room on main and on the line, the remedy's candidates there, and the three takes for the sub-pixel strips design

Step 3 of the Design X line's resume plan (t7-3e22, resume note of 2026-11-15). The takes decide the design of the remedy (t0-acxm) and give Alexis the facts for the decision on the sub-pixel strips design.

## Design

Written by the line orchestrator, 2026-11-21. This ticket starts when the other line's fix of the sun's shadow leak (t8-rjno) is on main and main is merged into the line's branch. It is an experiment: no change under src, tests or tools.

### Why

The room of scene orbit with the camera person is the scene of the still rect-lamps (the lamp hall), of the switch "cabin", of the walk, of the stand film and of the climb. Until the shadow fix, 99.2 % of its direct sunlight was a leak through the sun's shadow. The leak fed the bright specks of the ceiling, the 18-draw cycle of the cabin center r11 under the jitter, and the loss of light of every test that follows the jitter. So no acceptance number of that room counts until both sides are taken again. The shadow fix also changes the edge of every sun shadow, so the bay's film gets one new take too.

### The user's bar (Alexis, 2026-11-15)

Acceptance is by the engine's stills and films. They must look good and show no blotches, daubs, bright specks (fireflies), trail or flicker. A reference explains a fault and is not the bar. A little lost light is acceptable. The bay film's bar is: no worse than main. So each answer starts with what the pictures show, beside main, and the scores follow.

### The sides

- M = main at its head with the shadow fix.
- X = the line's head with main merged: light evidence of a ray cuts the bounce history to the fast cap of 4 draws and keeps the clamp box wide.
- The remedy's candidates, each X plus one switch of the scratch probe. The probe is docs/research/t0-ion0-region-drift/scratch-probe-on-73f4a6548.patch, ported to the line's head. The line's head holds the nearest-half store in src, so the probe's store switch goes away.
  - W4 = the regional lag test with the fast history of 4 draws as its recent mean, tolerance 10 % (TERTIUS_X_REGION=8 TERTIUS_X_REGION_TOL=10). It gave the best valid bay film of the lag tests (+0.89 / +0.43) and failed only in this room under the jitter.
  - Q3 and Q1 = the region's drift as a second source of light evidence (t0-ion0), at 3 and 2 standard deviations. Each closes the bay film's trail (+0.47 / +0.23 and +0.44 / +0.19).
  - C4 = every bounce history held at 4 draws, the control that closes the film and settles the switch.
- A = both clamps off, the reference. It explains a fault. It is not the bar.

### The takes

1. The still rect-lamps under today's jitter, at the automatic exposure and at 4.08 stops: M, X, W4, Q3, Q1, C4, A. The whole frame, and the ceiling crop at full resolution, side by side. Specks, blotch and daub. The light of ceiling and walls as a share of A, as a measure.
2. The switch "cabin" under today's jitter: M, X, W4, Q3, Q1, C4. The draws until the slowest region and r11 stay within 5 % of their own control, the level 60 draws after each switch, and r11's lowest and highest level over frames 140 to 159, which shows flicker before the switch. One chart of r11.
3. The stand film: M and X, and the candidate that leads after takes 1 and 2.
4. The film rect-bay and the still of its end state: M, X, and the candidate that leads. Level and blotch at the middle and the last frame, and one sheet of frames beside main.
5. For the sub-pixel strips design (t0-qwx8, t0-f238, t0-70v1), on M:
   - r11 of the single bounce at world ticks 300 and 306 under the 18 jitter phases. Before the fix r11 fell 43 % between the two ticks.
   - r11 per jitter phase in the switch's still state at tick 60, with the cycle's mean and the level without jitter. Also the lamps-on control with and without jitter for all 9 regions, because r11 lost 46 % without jitter while the whole image held within 0.2 % (t0-ion0, finding 4).
   - The census of young light-age records in the still room under the jitter (0.136 % of the pixels before the fix, 0 without jitter).

### The questions

1. By the user's bar, does X look good in this room after the shadow fix, beside M: the still, the switch and the stand film?
2. Which candidate keeps the still clean, settles the switch without flicker before it, and keeps the bay film no worse than main? If X alone does, say so: then the remedy is only what the bay film needs.
3. Does a measured case of a real sub-pixel strip remain after the fix: a step of r11 between ticks 300 and 306, a cycle of r11 over the jitter's phases, or young light-age records in a still?
