# Ceiling highlights form a regular grid on the first frames after a camera cut

Alexis sees repeated square groups on the sunlit ceiling after a real cut, on the earlier main and still with reflection guiding. In the committed ter-fe63 production nose strip, first-frame local brightness has a peak at 6 output pixels and another at 12. The 3 by 3 history fix at a history length of 1 uses spacing 4 render pixels, which predicts 6 at render scale 2/3. Separate its contribution from the 2 by 2 shared ray, guided-turn schedule and upscaler. Compare fixed-count deterministic reconstruction patterns against the original before choosing a production change.

## Stage 2 Production Design (Settled 2026-11-24)

### System design and ownership

- Authoritative draw-index path: `FrameHistory.PendingFrame.DrawIndex` (`long`) is already passed into `AccumulatePushConstants.Of(SignalParameters parameters, bool reset, long drawIndex)`. Add one field `public uint DrawIndex;` (`unchecked((uint)drawIndex)`, matching `ScreenTracePushConstants.DrawIndex` and `ToneMapPushConstants.DrawIndex`) to `AccumulatePushConstants` in `src/Tertius.Rendering/Scene/GpuTypes.cs` and `uint drawIndex;` to `AccumulatePush` in `src/Tertius.Rendering/Shaders/accumulate.glsl`. Keep `RegionSlot` unchanged so `region_drift.glsl` and `t1-yi11` share one push-constant struct without a second draw-index path.
- Blue-noise mask ownership and portable binding: `BrdfTableImages` remains the single owner of `BlueNoiseMask`. `FrameResources` already writes `tables.BlueNoise` unconditionally to set `0` binding `20` (`DescriptorLayouts.BlueNoiseBinding`, `ShaderStageFlags.ComputeBit`, not partially bound) for every frame in flight across all renderer configurations. `AccumulateLayout` includes `FrameSet` (set `0`), so `accumulate_spatial.comp` declares `layout(set = 0, binding = 20) uniform utexture2D blueNoiseMask;` and includes `"blue_noise.glsl"` with no descriptor layout or pool growth.
- Specular-only rotated short-history fix (`src/Tertius.Rendering/Shaders/accumulate_spatial.comp`, Step 3):
  - Apply rotated per-pixel stencils ONLY when `parameters.light == SpecularLight` and `spacing` is `4` or `2`. Keep `DiffuseLight` completely untouched (axis-aligned `spacing * ivec2(x, y)`, identical to `main`). Keep `spacing == 1` (and any spacing other than `4` or `2`) as the axis-aligned 3 x 3 square.
  - Constant integer offset tables (zero runtime trigonometry, zero sorting, exact 9 color samples per pixel = center + 4 unique symmetric pairs `+-u, +-v, +-(u+v), +-(u-v)`, zero first moment, bounded footprint `<= 6` px at spacing `4` and `<= 3` px at spacing `2`, matched RMS radius within `2%` at spacing `4` and `5%` at spacing `2`):
    - Spacing 4: 8 orientations (`0, 11.25, 22.5, 33.75, 45, 56.25, 67.5, 78.75 deg`), row 0 identical to the axis-aligned square.
    - Spacing 2: 4 orientations (`0, 26.57, 45, -26.57 deg`), row 0 identical to the axis-aligned square.
  - Distinct deterministic blue-noise phase: fetch `blueNoiseBytesOf(pixel, parameters.drawIndex + StencilBlueNoisePhase).r` where `const uint StencilBlueNoisePhase = BlueNoiseFrames / 2u;` (`32u`). Across all `uint` draw indices, `((drawIndex + 32u) & 63u)` is always 32 frames away from `(drawIndex & 63u)`, so the stencil never reuses a reflection ray's or tone-map dither's exact mask byte while keeping the 64-frame spatiotemporal progression. Use `byte >> 5u` at spacing `4` and `byte >> 6u` at spacing `2`.
  - Keep exact loop structure, `insideImages`, `sameSurface`, length weights (`other.a`), and `reflectionGuideScratch` weighting unchanged.
