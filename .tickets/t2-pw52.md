# A reflection walk of at most 96 steps: 0.15 ms on the deck

Alexis chose a reflection step cap of 96 on 2026-11-12. The choice followed the step cap search of the frame-time sweep and its two pictures of the mirror balls (docs/research/frame-time-sweep-2026-11-11/addendum.md, section A13).

The change: ScreenTrace.Default sets ReflectionSteps to 96 in place of 128. The bounce-light cap stays at 62.

The search measured on main c87eac88f: the deck's median frame span as a window falls by 0.21 ms, from 19.75 to 19.54 ms, in four traces per cap. At a fixed exposure, 128 stills of 32 scripts change by at most 0.060 % in the mean. The mottle score does not move. The rims of mirror balls get a little fainter again, and FLIP flags up to 0.08 % of a still there.

Main moved after the search: ter-s97m changed the random numbers of the reflection rays. So the acceptance of this ticket runs the stills, the film and the timing again on the code that merges.

Done when:

- The options test names 96, and decision 41 and ARCHITECTURE.md follow.
- The mutants of the changed line are dead, and both suites pass.
- One workbench comparison and the Mac numbers are in the note.
- The branch is merged and pushed.
