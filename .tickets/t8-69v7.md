# A still's diffuse light depends on the diffuse history's length: a cap of 4 draws adds 4 to 7 % with the diffuse filter and removes 15 % without it

Found 2026-11-14 by t0-y46f (docs/research/t0-y46f-window-18-excess/README.md, section "Question 3").

In a still, the long mean of an exponential history of unbiased samples must not depend on the history's length. The scratch switch TERTIUS_X_DIFFUSE_CAP=<N> caps the diffuse history's length at N draws at every pixel, with no test. Lamp hall still rect-lamps at 4.08 stops, no jitter, both clamps off (TERTIUS_A_NO_CLAMP=1), light of the long mean as % of the same build with the cap of 32 draws, ceiling / walls:

- cap 4: 104.2 / 106.8. Whole image 104.1 %. The bounce signal alone: 104.3 / 107.9, whole image 105.2 %.
- cap 8: 103.7 / 106.0. cap 16: 102.5 / 103.9.
- cap 4 over 5,900 draws (long mean of draws 5,313 to 5,940): 105.0 / 107.1 against the cap of 32 over the same draws. So the gap is steady, not a slow rise of the long history.
- cap 4 with the diffuse filter off (TERTIUS_DIFFUSE_FILTER=off): 84.6 / 84.5 against the cap of 32 with the filter off. The sign changes.
- Cabin still exit-cabin at 1.0 stops: cap 4 gives 101.8 % of the whole smooth image and 102.0 % of r11.

The clamps, the own rays and the ray weight cap do not cause it (t0-y46f takes B, D, E and Acap). The engine cuts a diffuse history to the fast cap of 4 draws after light evidence, so a young history after a light change carries this bias in its first draws. Find the length-dependent step in the loop of the accumulation, the diffuse filter's feedback into the slow history, and the rays that read the filtered light at their hits.
