# Expose the first displayed frame of every camera cut for its new view

A cut or initial camera activation must use the new view’s own meter reading on its first displayed frame. Continuous movement of the same camera through changing light keeps gradual adaptation. Exercise a genuine cut between nearby poses as well as a large change of viewpoint.

## Acceptance Criteria

Compare cuts from dark to bright and bright to dark against the settled automatic exposure of each destination at the same pose. Check the first displayed frame in the film and in a window, including a return to a previously used camera. A continuous walk through the hatch must still adapt over time. Fixed manual exposure must keep its selected value. Use seated-outside at 28.60 s and pen-on-the-pad at 29.35 s.
