# Find why distant lights boil during motion

Track a distant light whose footprint or brightness fluctuates from frame to frame. Inspect the hub approach and the illuminated rim terminal for a repeatable example. Record the first confirmed timestamp, source and screen size; the large white discs from the earlier backlog have a separate, unconfirmed cause.

## Acceptance Criteria

Track the same light across consecutive frames with fixed exposure under stationary and slow-moving cameras. Measure integrated brightness, apparent area and position. Compare the emitting surface or impostor before bloom, temporal reconstruction and the final image. Correct the first unstable stage while preserving the brightness and motion of the source.
